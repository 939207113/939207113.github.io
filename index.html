About Me

个人简介

I am a Ph.D. student in Materials Science and Engineering in the joint doctoral training program between Zhejiang University and Westlake University. My research focuses on computational materials science, with particular interests in machine-learning-assisted materials discovery, solid-state ionics, crystal structure generation, and ferroelectric materials.
我是浙江大学与西湖大学联合培养的材料科学与工程博士研究生。主要从事计算材料学研究，研究兴趣包括机器学习辅助材料发现、固态离子学、晶体结构生成与铁电材料等。
